original Portuguese version is preserved verbatim at

- [`TASKS.md`](TASKS.md) for dependency order, acceptance criteria, and
  evidence;
- [`docs/decisions/`](docs/decisions/) for accepted design and experimental

second 1,030-line plan. The active sources above capture the implemented
architecture and later amendments. Consult the archived plan only for
historical rationale, never to override an accepted ADR or the current task.
