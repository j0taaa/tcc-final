\section{Complementary Commitment Certificates}
\label{sec:budgeted}

Sampling and proposal preservation are different objectives. The retained MWPC
backend maximizes matched positive proposal reward. With free positions
$I=\{i:x_i=\mask\}$ and $r_i(a)=\sum_{j:i_j=i,t_j=a,w_j>0}w_j$, its budgeted
extension computes
\begin{equation}
 \operatorname{OPT}_B=\max_{y\in\Feas_x,\ T\subseteq I,\ |T|\leq B}
 \sum_{i\in T}r_i(y_i).
 \label{eq:budget-objective}
\end{equation}
The budget counts positions, preserving positive duplicate IDs. For a fixed
witness, its largest $B$ rewards are optimal; maximizing this value over witnesses
gives the joint optimum. Selecting confidence before optimization or filtering
an unbudgeted witness afterwards can have vanishing approximation ratios, as
shown by the infinite families preserved in \path{paper/budgeted-math.tex}.

\begin{theorem}[Budget frontier and certificate]
Paid/unpaid token closings compile Equation~\ref{eq:budget-objective} into a
finite CFG-on-DAG resource program. Complete unpruned inference computes every
budget optimum and witness. Max-plus upper potentials satisfying all epsilon,
terminal and binary relaxation inequalities bound every accepted path; a valid
witness attaining the bound proves optimality. Absent bounds for all permissible
roots prove support infeasibility.
\label{thm:budget-certificate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
A paid closing contributes one position and its aggregated reward; every
feasible witness/position pair has the same resource path. Induction on
acyclic epsilon paths and topological CFG spans establishes complete inference
and the potential upper bound. Witness attainment gives equal lower and upper
bounds. The detailed recurrence, certificate and separation proofs remain in
\path{paper/budgeted-math.tex} and \path{paper/supplement-resource-dp.tex}.
\end{proof}

The independent checker verifies original tokens, fixed slots, complete support
and EOS/PAD correspondence before evaluating potentials. A common-input EPIC
batch with the same support, positions and budget is a feasible competitor, so
its reward cannot exceed the joint optimum; native decoding may have different
inputs and no accuracy comparison follows. Classical implicit-hitting-set
conflict search \cite{davies2011maxhs,saikko2016lmhs} supplies alternative upper
bounds and safe conflict/witness reuse. These are complementary retained APIs,
not implementations of the exact posterior law in Section~\ref{sec:cfg-posterior}.
