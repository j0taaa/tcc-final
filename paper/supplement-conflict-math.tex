With $h$ applicable conflicts, master depth is at most $h$, each branch has at
most $B$ children, and each node scans/sorts $m$ rewarded choices. Learning $H$
conflicts costs at most $(B+1)H+1$ feasibility queries, including deletions.
Along $T$ retained-support solves with budgets at most $B_{\max}$, the bound is
$T+(B_{\max}+1)H$. Large conflict sets can still be expensive. In the fixed
language $\{ab,ba\}$ with two high-weight $a$ proposals and budget two, cold
solving uses $4T$ queries; retaining its forbidden $aa$ pair and valid $ab$
witness uses four total over all score-scaled solves. This is a computation
separation, not an empirical occurrence estimate. Full proofs are in
\path{docs/research/m29-conflict-commitment.md}; Lean checks the stated cover,
transport and query-count specifications.
