\subsection{Exact commitment through certified conflicts}
\label{sec:conflicts}

Implicit hitting sets and SAT--optimization separation are established
\cite{davies2011maxhs,saikko2016lmhs}. We specialize this principle to physical
token commitments, keeping rational rewards and original-input certificates.
Let $C$ be a set of position/token choices with no witness in $\Feas_x$.
Every feasible batch must omit a member of $C$. A master maximizes the budgeted
reward while excluding certified conflicts; a finite-slot CFG oracle tests its
candidate. Rejected candidates are reduced by exact deletion tests and given
independently checked zero-budget resource infeasibility proofs.

For excluded choices $E$, the sum $U(E)$ of the $B$ largest allowed
per-position maxima bounds every remaining batch. When a relaxed optimum
contains conflict $C$, the children $E\cup\{c\}$, for all $c\in C$, cover every
feasible parent batch. Leaves record $U(E)$ and branches record the maximum
child bound. An empty conflict proves infeasibility. The original-input checker
verifies this complete cover and each conflict proof without running the master.

\begin{theorem}[Conflict-guided exactness and reusable certification]
A feasible master optimum is an $\operatorname{OPT}_B$ witness. Certified
conflicts remain sound under completion-set contraction. A stored full witness
attaining the current master bound certifies optimality without a new CFG-oracle
query, even after changed rewards or support expansion, provided the witness
passes the new original-input validation.
\label{thm:conflict-reuse}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every genuine feasible batch obeys the certified conflicts, so the master is an
upper bound. A checked witness supplies its matching lower bound. The
member-complete branching cover proves the same bound by induction. If
$\Feas_{x'}\subseteq\Feas_x$, a choice set with no witness in the latter has none
in the former; already fixed matching members can be discharged. Positive
witness transport instead requires direct validation in the new support and
fixed canvas. Recomputing the current reward and master bound then gives the
same sandwich. No score-stability or semantic premise is assumed.
\end{proof}

The query bound is $T+(B_{\max}+1)H$ for $T$ retained-support solves and $H$
learned conflicts, with at most $B_{\max}$ deletion queries per conflict.
Full termination, complexity and computation-separation proofs are in
\path{docs/research/m29-conflict-commitment.md}. Lean checks cover, transport
and query-count specifications; it does not prove the implementation source.
