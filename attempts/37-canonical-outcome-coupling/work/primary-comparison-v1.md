## Read sources and comparison

- **CARMS2021**, original Sections3/3.1/3.2.1/3.2.2 and Algorithms1/2 read:
  [NeurIPS PDF](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/file/6e16656a6ee1de7232164767ccfa7920-Paper.pdf).
  SHA256 `6b0d1ed4eae5444777abe36c57764732908abdad01461e494ad6686b43fd3170`.
  Categorical antithetics and preserving marginals are precedents. Its CDF/
  Gumbel constructions use probabilities or logits; gradient estimators also
  account for the pair law. Do not claim invention of categorical antithetics.
- **Jafari Jozani/Perron**, original Sections1--4 read, with the present
  comparison rereading Sections1--3 and Theorem2.1:
  [authored report](https://www.usherbrooke.ca/mathematiques/fileadmin/sites/mathematiques/documents/Recherche/Publications/Rapports_de_recherche/rr64.pdf).
  SHA256 `8e7a94cb5f672bddf62b79ccc5fd6c6eadb5d7a427c3fe77e3bfe7dfd55ba097`.
  Cheap ranking/expensive measurement and ranked-set variance reductions
  already exist. Their binary four-item law and efficient early-stop control
  must not be weakened. Random canonical classes do not presume that the
  correct outcome is extreme in a semantic ranking.
- **Antithetic Noise in Diffusion Models**, original
  [v2 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.06185v2), Sections1--3.2 read.
  Pairing Gaussian noise preserves each marginal with no extra generation.
  Its score-symmetry statement is a conjecture supported by images, not a
  bound for every canonical discrete outcome and every complete decoder.
  This is nevertheless a serious cheaper comparator, not excluded merely
  because its theorem has another hypothesis. This reading does not cover
  the entire appendix or assert no similar result anywhere in that paper.
- **GLIDE2026**, original Sections2--4.4 read:
  [original PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.31278).
  SHA256 `da95ca7c47a0d4072737014a7539d238df194858709eaf56303ac820c4354933`.
  Prediction-powered/stratified/active evaluation already addresses costly
  trajectory annotation. A37 cannot claim to invent that application or
  ignore uniform sampling without replacement from a prepared pool. Different
  estimators may use unbiased weighting instead of original output marginals.
- **Diffusion Language Model Inference with Monte Carlo Tree Search**, original
  AppendixA.1--A.4 read for the antithetic hit and cost comparison:
  [ACL PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-eacl.180.pdf).
  SHA256 `38d5d95a16e5deace2918a3662b824198e318b3cfd6c24cc07e271e5ed310269`.
  The antithetic reference there concerns LLaDA1.5/VRPO. Its actual search
  method is an inference-time compute/quality control, not a proof of universal
  oracle coupling. MCTS/best-of-more remain legitimate controls when the
  application does not need the original marginal law.

## Direct reduction tests still required

The barrier-three binary law is classical ruin; the bounded-four control is
Nacu--Peres2005 Proposition24, already credited in A32/A34. Random linear
hashing and canonicalization are established techniques. The only candidate
own distinction is the uniform canonical-event guarantee and its exact
constant/conditions, paired with a useful consumer of complete dLLM outputs.

Searches for random-partition antithetics, black-box categorical antithetics,
hash/coupling diversity and unknown-probability sampling did not identify an
exact reduction in the sources actually inspected. That is NOT a completed
priority audit. Need attack with simpler oracle-only designs, arbitrary scores,
several correct canonical outcomes and full-cost controls before promotion.
No reviewer was contacted and no publication sufficiency is confirmed.
