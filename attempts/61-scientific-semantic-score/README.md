# A61 — A scientific-value score for a frozen dLLM numerical field

2026-10-17. Eight criteria BEFORE prototype, head or training. This is a new
TRAINING/assessment operation, not a repair of A60's failed generic capacity
justification, A58's closed kernel gate, or a trained dJev model.

1. **Real operation.** Train/evaluate a numerical predictive distribution from
   one frozen factorized dLLM head conditioned on a JSON-numeral language. A
   code-to-metric consumer uses numerical uncertainty across very different
   scales (runtime/memory/etc.). RLM for Code2026 documents that consumer and
   uses a frozen encoder plus numerical decoding. This experiment has NOT
   acquired its profiled labels or validated a dLLM binding. A source path has
   fixed ORIGINAL token slots/IDs, with all admitted spellings and aliases;
   the score sees the numerical value, not its arbitrary textual spelling.
2. **Specific candidate.** Log score of semantic sign/decimal ORDER plus CRPS
   of normalized significand CONDITIONED ON THE OBSERVED semantic category.
   The possible contribution is an exact native-token, whole-value objective
   and its useful effect, not a new proper-scoring theorem. CRPS, conditional
   scores, scientific codecs, digit EMD, rational kernels, differentiation,
   normalizer cancellation and sparse projection are antecedents. A generic
   complete WFST/min-kernel implementation computes the SAME objective; do not
   claim own generic inference novelty or use it as a different quality method.
3. **Why choose it.** Potential semantic calibration and within-order numerical
   learning without target min/max normalization, output-magnitude explosion
   or sampled/Gumbel score bias. Written consequences: strict propriety under
   its stated population assumptions, decimal-rescaling invariance, bounded
   per-row logit gradients, polynomial exact rational components/gradients.
   These are candidate application properties, not a measured quality/speed
   win. Mandatory alternative objectives include raw/canonical-completion CE,
   complete semantic NLL, NTL/NTIL/SMMD, scientific-codec CE/conditional scores,
   and strong direct probabilistic regression heads on equal information.
   Ordinary CRPS is a DIFFERENT score, so its bit barrier is not a same-task
   speedup claim. No all-method advantage is currently established.
4. **Proof first.** work/theory-v1.md will give definitions, population-risk
   identity, exact selected-order/min-kernel construction, full bit/cost bounds,
   source-logit gradient bounds, and the explicit strong canonicalization control.
   The candidate is not discovery of conditional CRPS or kernel composition.
5. **First validation.** Only a tiny independent rational source reference:
   original paths/aliases, signed zero/extreme exponents, exact analytic logit
   derivatives against independent dual enumeration, proper-risk identities,
   scaling and gradient bounds. Its source must be committed before execution.
   No model/head/update or numerical-quality gate is triggered by these tests.
   A real training protocol needs pinned inputs, complete controls, costs,
   independent evaluation and success/refusal criteria BEFORE new captures.
6. **Complete costs/scope.** Charge original-token expansion, grammar/order
   evidence, both copies in the min-kernel, all forward/backward/rational work,
   full head/backbone/optimizer and output. Small reference may ENUMERATE tiny
   paths; it is not the polynomial compiler. Fixed-slot conditional loss cancels
   common full-vocabulary row normalizers, but variable EOT with marginalized
   arbitrary suffixes may STILL REQUIRE full-vocabulary sums. No sparse-head
   benefit is assumed for that native contract. This is one head's predictive
   law, not final iterative-decoder likelihood or guaranteed calibration after SGD.
7. **Fatal objections.** The objective may be a direct known specialization with
   no substantial useful difference. Canonicalization already repairs the toy
   alias example; semantic NLL is already proper and often much cheaper. A
   scientific codec/probe may be better than preserving arbitrary native text.
   Decimal-order boundaries are artificial and wrong orders get classification
   loss rather than a numerical distance. Decimal rescaling is not arbitrary
   physical-unit invariance. Exact proper loss does not guarantee good training.
   No artificial consumer restriction may exclude these strong controls.
8. **Article obligations.** Specific novelty/significance and real useful effect
   remain OPEN. Need an actual available code/number consumer, complete native
   integration and fair real evaluation. Do not call classical proofs, toy risk
   reductions, numerical correctness or a resource screen publication/master's
   success. No trained model, new head/forward/update, human review or positive
   goal completion is asserted. If no own useful consequence survives controls,
   retain this audit and change direction instead of growing the implementation.
