Current V5 materializes the [single-preparation upper-profile proof](work/upper-v5.md)

The [primary audit](work/primary-comparison-v5.md) credits Dyer2003 and the

2026-10-13. Mathematical contract BEFORE implementation or observations.
This is a candidate, not confirmed priority, publication sufficiency, an EPIC
win or a measured faster dJev. A49 V29's downgrade and A24's separate evidence
remain unchanged. There is no new neural campaign.

1. **Use.** Correct a valid draft completion from one frozen dLLM head into
   the exact grammatical posterior of another, or couple two such heads for
   comparison. After rejection the required law is (P-Q)_+/TV(P,Q), not P
   alone. Inputs are the two heads, the same original-ID support/canvas and
   an admitted unambiguous CFG forest. A software caller consumes a complete
   original-token assignment, with exact target marginal and validity.
2. **Significance.** Maximal coupling, speculative acceptance, approximate
   counting, monotone sparsification and rejection correction are OLD. The
   candidate is a complete polynomial-bit algorithm for the grammar residual,
   with conditional rejection cost independent of how small TV is. Its
   difference from known generic approximation frameworks remains to be
   audited; merely instantiating one is insufficient scientific novelty.
3. **Choice.** Jacob/O'Leary/Atchade2020 Algorithm2 costs 1/TV target draws
   conditional on its residual branch, despite constant UNCONDITIONAL mean.
   The proposed residual proposal needs at most two correction attempts in
   expectation, each with fully charged polynomial work. Enumeration, exact
   ratio envelopes, adaptive OS*/CARS, categorical residuals and counting-based
   constructions are mandatory competent controls. No universal latency or
   unconditional-mean superiority is asserted.
4. **Without code.** [The written construction](work/theory-v1.md) uses a
   relative ALL-threshold survival profile of likelihood ratios, preserving
   exact zero and avoiding subtraction of two nearly equal approximations.
   Approximate prefix masses give a pointwise proposal bound; exact rejection
   then restores the residual law. The potential contribution is the full
   operation/cost theorem, not the individual classical lemmas.
5. **Validation.** First independent exhaustive tails, products, prefix masses
   and accepted-law reconstruction on small original-token JSON/stack cases.
   Include zero supports/masses, equal laws, aliases, prefixes and tiny rational
   differences. These are mathematical counterexample searches, not favorable
   performance benchmarks. No model measurement before proof/prior audit.
6. **Cost.** Grammar preparation, both exact normalizers, input/scaling bits,
   each profile construction, every proposed token and exact correction count.
   V1 pays n*q profile queries PER attempt; V4 reduces this to n; V5 uses ONE
   preparation and pays O(H*M^2) provenance storage. The algorithm does not save
   the forward needed to obtain a target head. Its worst polynomial may be
   impractical, and expensive preprocessing is not hidden behind rare events.
7. **Objection.** The theorem may follow directly from #Knapsack approximation
   and self-reducible sampling; a stronger residual envelope may already be
   cheap. A49 TV intervals do not automatically provide relative prefix
   residual masses. Whole-completion correction is distinct from the law of
   a value-dependent partial commitment policy or EPIC's native decoder.
8. **Publication.** Need a genuinely distinct result, full proof/bit validation
   and an operational consequence surviving competent controls. Human review,
   priority and practical superiority remain unconfirmed. If the direct known
   construction matches this theorem, preserve it and continue the search.
