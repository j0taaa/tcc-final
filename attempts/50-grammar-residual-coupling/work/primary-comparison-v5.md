# Primary comparison and current decision — A50 V5

2026-10-13. This is an internal audit, not independent human review or
confirmed scientific priority. Earlier failures/refinements stay immutable.

## The operation, not a new maximal-coupling identity

Given two original-ID product laws conditioned on the same admitted compact
grammatical event forest, sample EXACTLY from (P-Q)_+/TV(P,Q) after a rejected
draft. V5 uses one preparation and provenance, not n*q prefix recompilations.
Its written bound is polynomial preparation/bit storage and polynomial work
per output in expectation, with fewer than two conditional correction attempts.
This is a useful mathematical sampling primitive. It has not been shown to
make an entire dLLM generation cheaper than sampling its target directly.

The Halman2019 automatic-FPTAS original was also examined for its calculus

the 2018 sum-approximation lemma DOES. Do not claim that noticing this gap
isolates substantial novelty: the competent combined construction already
has the missing algebra.

## What is and is not established

Written proofs and independent law checks support a concrete operation:
an upper-profile residual sampler with original IDs, singular supports,
pointwise rejection and a conditional attempt bound independent of1/TV.
V5's oracle completes475,041 upper-tail checks,6,480 real tail merges,
1,780 complete word cases,18 proposal/accepted laws,6 singular laws,
432 valid samples and12 undefined-posterior refusals. Twelve actual grammatical
words have strictly increased representatives, so the law audit exercises
compression, not just an enumeration in disguise. These are correctness
checks; the fixtures are not performance evidence or proof of novelty.

The entire substantial difference for publication is STILL OPEN. A competent
reader can combine Dyer's preparation/rejection principle, 2018's profile
algebra and ordinary provenance on a deterministic decomposable forest.
The complete weighted positive-part theorem and its application must be
judged against that construction, not against an intentionally weak sampler.
We do not claim that no previous paper contains this corollary. A targeted
search returning no matching title does not establish novelty.

No large model or timing campaign is authorized by this pass alone. First
close the 2019 sampling/construction comparison, fully account grammar and
provenance costs, and identify an operation that benefits after those costs.
An honest sampler theorem may be a TCC result; the user's stronger scientific
and useful-generation objective has not yet been declared achieved.
