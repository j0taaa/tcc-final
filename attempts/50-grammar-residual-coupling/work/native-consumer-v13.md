## Contract and eight criteria

1. Use: correct a cached valid JSON completion after a real dLLM canvas update,
   preserving the exact frozen grammatical target posterior at that update.
   The consumer returns original token IDs and their JSON bytes. Both heads
   are actual MDLM predictions at different canvases, not a probability table
   rounded solely to create a correction task.
2. Significance: the classical residual algorithm is already credited in V11.
   Its compact CFG realization and operational economics must be judged
   separately. Functional validity is not novelty or semantic accuracy.
3. Advantage: maximum possible probability of retaining the complete draft
   under the declared marginal contract. This is a classical mathematical
   guarantee. Any speed claim would additionally need direct target sampling,
   exact scalar coupling, marginal-grid coupling and other competent controls
   on the same operation, with all costs. This screen makes NO speed claim.
4. Proof: V7 supplies the legal conditioning/induction argument. Choose the
   leftmost free position BEFORE sampling. Fix its original ID from the draft.
   Conditional on that visible update, the cached draft has the old head
   conditioned on the new fixed token. A new model head then defines P.
   Accept cached X with min(1,P(X)/Q(X)); after rejection use exact(P-Q)_+/TV.
   This returns P and maximizes whole-original-word retention. All normalizers
   refer to the newly restricted state. No future unobserved sample determines
   the chosen commit position.
5. Cases: ALL15 archived A49 V25 frames from ALL five eligible pin-identified
   JSONTestSuite documents and mask counts4/8/16. The full-vocabulary and
   top16 support variants both enter, making30 initial rows. top16 is frozen
   from the OLD head by descending probability, ties by original ID. The new
   head cannot expand it; its omitted support is declared. No correct answer
   or timing chooses an input or support. This cohort is KNOWN development,
   not heldout. Every initial failure/zero mass and downstream failure stays.
6. Cost/resources: count old archive reading, original byte vocabulary,
   source normalization/admission/compilation, exact old posterior and sample,
   actual new forward/full softmax, both new conditional normalizers,
   accepted-draft density and residual preparation/output. Diagnostic clocks
   are not comparative latency. Initial CPU subprocess:30s wall,3GiB virtual
   memory,200,000 chart cells/1,000,000 alternatives,5,000,000 preprocessing
   work;45s supervisor. Corrector:30s CPU-stage deadline and at most4,096
   residual attempts, returning FAIL rather than an approximate sample.
   Every method is subject to the same screen limits; no win is credited to
   another method's refusal. Dense logits/arrays are retained locally with
   hashes and exact original-token bytes. Model startup is recorded separately.
7. Objections: whole-completion retention does not minimize edits of only the
   committed subset; direct P sampling may be cheaper. top16 may exclude every
   valid completion, and full vocabulary may exceed the current adapter's
   limits. These are failures of this realization, not zero TV or a proof
   that all CFG algorithms need explicit stacks. Fixed tokens/aliases must
   remain literal. Selection based on sampled-token confidence would invalidate
   V7's cached-draft law and is not substituted here.
8. Publication: valid corrected JSON and real heads close only the functional
   insertion gap. Novelty, useful paid speed/efficiency, semantic quality,
   independent human proof review and publication remain OPEN. The main
   article remains A24. Do not transfer any screen observation into its claims.
