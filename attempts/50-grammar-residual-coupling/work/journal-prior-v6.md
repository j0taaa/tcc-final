## Consequence for the novelty objection

The paper explicitly retains back-pointers in a compressed DP, prepares the
representation ONCE and generates subsequent outputs by following those
pointers. It proves a pointwise near-uniform output guarantee and accounts
random bits, storage and preparation separately. These are direct ancestors
of V1's sequential proposal and V5's retained-provenance sampling.

This removes the earlier missing-source objection. It does NOT invalidate
the weighted grammatical residual theorem, because the journal's sampler is
near-uniform knapsack generation, not (P-Q)_+ on an original-token forest.
But neither one preparation nor back-pointers are a distinguishing invention.
Combining this source with Dyer2003 and the2018 sum/convolution calculus is
a strong competing construction. The elementary rational product-coordinate
adaptation and exact rejection are not, alone, substantial novelty.

Current decision: preserve A50 as a validated mathematical residual primitive,
keep the user objective OPEN, and judge the complete weighted-forest operation
and a paid practical application against that combined construction. Do not
start a model campaign to replace this priority question with a favorable
timing. No claim of publication sufficiency or faster dJev is made here.
