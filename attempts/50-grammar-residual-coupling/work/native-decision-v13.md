## Decision

The mathematical consumer can be materialized on actual heads. Coverage and
resource costs do not support a deployable full-vocabulary dJev advantage.
The profile did not demonstrate practical superiority, and the scalar core
is a classical specialization under V11's stronger comparison. Do not promote
A50 to the main article or declare the publication/usefulness goal achieved.
Keep all references and evidence. Further implementation must solve a
concrete surviving bottleneck or provide a distinct result before another
competitive campaign; finite correctness counts alone cannot do that.
