* [DiffuSeq2210.08933v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.08933v3), Section3
  Training and Inference Methods READ. The authors already use full diffusion

  MBR to a diffusion language generator is not new in general. This is not
  evidence that they implement this exact native terminal numeric readout.
* [Jinnai et al., ICML2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/jinnai24a.html),
  ORIGINAL proceedings ABSTRACT READ. Reweighting sampled hypotheses with
  available model probabilities is a known alternative to empirical counts;
  it remains a competent control when computing those probabilities is valid.
  No claim that raw independent logits equal the native terminal kernel.
* [Tromble et al., EMNLP2008](https://aclanthology.org/D08-1065.pdf),
  ORIGINAL first-page abstract/introduction text READ. Compact weighted
  lattices for MBR and loss-dependent tractability predate this work. No
  generic invention of compact Bayes-risk inference is available here.
* [Li/Eisner, EMNLP2009](https://aclanthology.org/D09-1005.pdf), ORIGINAL
  first-page abstract/introduction text READ. First/second moments and
  derivatives on lattices/forests via expectation semirings are predecessors;
  the A65 sum-state/moment control is a classical composition. Remaining
  sections were not audited as a source of a specific numeric-grid algorithm.
* [Structure-Conditional MBR2510.20700v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20700v1),
  author ABSTRACT only READ. Its structure-sensitive utilities concern groups
  of full responses; no generic structure-aware MBR priority claim here.
