not a fabricated sampler benchmark. Its source includes a subtle terminal
law: converge on constrained marginals, then reproject THOSE log-marginals

state paths. A future hook must reproduce that exact native transition and
filter to completions of the whole response. It may not silently use the
original head product or average raw numbers before normalization.
