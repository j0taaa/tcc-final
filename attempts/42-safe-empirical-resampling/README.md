## Eight scientific decision questions

1. **Use.** Two expensive evaluations of original noising states of a fixed
   CFG-valid JSON/DSL candidate, with more cheap proxy/classification proposals.
   A reusable scorer or stochastic gradient estimator needs exact original
   marginals and protection against an inaccurate proxy. No new NN training,
   rule learner, exact sequence likelihood or semantic success claim.
2. **Difference.** A41's sharp all-budget unknown-class ATOM frontier is not
   arbitrary-score safe under its grouped half-rotation. Its B8 union
   counterexample is preserved. Investigate simultaneous attainment of that
   frontier AND the all-score Loewner guarantee for every budget. Conditional
   Poisson/rejective/maximum-entropy sampling is OLD; it is the explicit
   strongest objection, not the claimed new principle. Priority remains open.
3. **Why choose.** Only if a universal theorem and useful full cost survive:
   retaining the paid first evaluation, two expensive original evaluations,
   better atom risk than cap4 without risking any gradient projection versus
   iid2. Competent retained RSS, analytic CV/inverse, same-law rejective,
   pivotal and cheap source derangement remain allowed and can win. A41's
   measured weak fixed proxy does NOT justify a new large neural campaign.
4. **Without code.** Define a known-address uniform source, unknown labels,
   and a uniform B-source pool. Use a known maximum-entropy no-repeat
   categorical coupling at empirical class probabilities, then lift back to
   original sources. Conditional correctness/atom optimality are relatively
   easy; unconditional arbitrary-score covariance is the UNCERTAIN step.
   A written conjecture is not a theorem. Search for counterexamples first.
5. **Validation.** Small exhaustive population/count-state calculation only,
   with numerical eigenvalue search followed by exact or certified checks
   for any obstruction. This is a proof aid, NOT a favourable model benchmark.
   No timing, independent corpus or neural protocol until proof/priority/use.
6. **Costs.** B cheap raw states/labels, nonlinear coupling-parameter solution,
   categorical/original-ID selection, bits, proxy fitting, two NN calls and
   output. Irrational max-entropy parameters need certified exact sampling
   before any exact implementation claim. Do not hide the calibration solve.
7. **Objection.** Conditional covariance safety need NOT survive the random
   pool's own mean fluctuation. Rejective/two-phase survey sampling may already
   supply the whole result. Old A39 opacity bounds do not apply when masses,
   analytical inverses or transparent mask structure are free. Stop or revise
   if a counterexample/reduction removes the distinction; preserve it.
8. **Publication gap.** Need the distinctive unconditional theorem, closest
   actual primary sources, complete cost and a practical CFG/dLLM operation
   with benefit. None is claimed completed. Main article and APIs unchanged.
