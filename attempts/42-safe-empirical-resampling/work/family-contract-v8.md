# V8: audit the entire six-pool anonymous atom-optimal family

Written before a new prototype. V7's exact failed blend and small-repair
limitation are preserved; no model campaign is justified by them.

The eight criteria of V6 apply with the following explicit re-evaluation.
1. Operation stays two original evaluations/continuations after cheap IID
   proposals from a fixed dLLM/CFG state. No semantic or EPIC timing claim.
2. Instead of another matching implementation, investigate a CHARACTERIZATION
   theorem: which anonymous, conditionally atom-optimal six-source couplings
   remain covariance-safe after a random IID pool? Classical pivotal/rank
   methods are within the family, not competitors weakened for a victory.
3. A useful result must delimit safe design freedom and the sharp query
   frontier, with a meaningful original application; classifying a tiny
   polynomial alone may still be insufficient for publication.
4. Matching6's count table fixes every profile except(2,2,1,1). Let t be
   the small/small joint probability there: 0<=t<=1/6; big/big=1/6+t;
   big/small=1/12-t/2. These all have marginal r/6 and atom-optimal diagonals.
   Derive the whole unconditional joint matrix as an affine polynomial in t.
   Safety for every t would follow from safety of both endpoints, but this
   is a CONJECTURE. Seek an exact adverse profile first.
5. Numerical eigenvalues are only obstruction search, followed by rational
   counterexamples. Freeze producer/seed/profile range before computation.
   No model benchmark, synthetic performance win or corpus selection.
6. Candidate law costs six source draws/classifications, matching/table
   selection with rational integer weights and two expensive evaluations.
   Class masses, known inverses/CV and exact native costs remain accounted.
7. It may simply subsume a known pivotal/ordered-overrelaxation theorem;
   unsafe endpoints would delimit rather than prove broad usefulness. No
   priority conclusion follows from a numerical screen without obstructions.
8. Pure universal proof/characterization, prior audit and significant CFG use
   still required. No main-article/API change, human review or publication
   acceptance claim follows from this minimal research aid.
