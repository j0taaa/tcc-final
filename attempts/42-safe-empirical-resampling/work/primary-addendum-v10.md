# Additional primary-source boundaries, 2026-10-11

These readings narrow novelty claims; they do not certify priority.

- Cytrynbaum/Savje, *Coupling Designs for Randomized Experiments with Complex
  Treatments*, arXiv:2604.09858v1, sections3,5.1/5.2,6.3/7.1:
  <https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09858v1>. The paper already treats general
  treatment spaces, an L2 coupling operator, eigenspace-dependent efficiency
  and robust/minimax comparisons. Its setup supplies the treatment marginal
  and transport maps. Therefore arbitrary-vector covariance analysis and
  application to complex spaces are NOT sufficient novelty. The finite
  retained unknown-source budget construction is a distinction from the
  examined sections, not proof the broader literature lacks it.
- Li/Chen/Huang, *Correlated Chance Sampling for Monte Carlo Counterfactual
  Regret Minimization*, arXiv:2607.27035v1, related work and appendicesA/D:
  <https://arxiv.org/html/2607.27035v1>. Persistent per-chance-node sequences
  use known cumulative probabilities; adaptive coefficients can have either
  covariance sign. Its per-traversal-reset theorem must not be confused with
  its per-iteration experimental design. Per-node coordination is an
  antecedent, not an original dLLM/CFG contribution here.
- Hashemi/Luo/Makur's49-page2026 antithetic preprint is listed at
  <https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/amakur/publications.html> and
  <https://zhankunluo.com/publication/antithetic/>. No full manuscript obtained;
  the author page supplies no PDF link for it. Priority cannot be ruled out
  using the different2025 slides/manuscript.
