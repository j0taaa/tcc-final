# Native first-prefix audit: do not promote the root coupling

All four prior input-only public cases were executed, with eight native
sources each and the first two full continuations. No semantic gold was
imported, no case selected by success and no upstream generator modified.
These are OLD development inputs with new native forwards, not an independent
evaluation. The serial diagnostic is not a live coupling implementation.

In all32 observed first yields the root operator is not yet concrete. To
reach it takes7--12 ADDITIONAL native model forwards per source. This is
near the end, not inexpensive resampling from one frozen head. Six of the
eight requested full continuations are valid executable JSON; two are
incomplete/invalid JSON and remain in the evidence. No quality win is claimed.

The information problem and the cost problem must both be resolved. Reaching
a label correlated with semantics late in a native trajectory does not make
that label a cheap proposal feature. A live pool would additionally need its
large tensors retained or a selected-source replay; the serial diagnostic
does not account that cost and cannot stand in for a competitive benchmark.

Useful universal cost sanity check (a classical Frechet consequence): for
two outputs each with success probability p, any dependence can yield at
most min(1,2p) success, versus2p-p^2 for iid2. Their ratio is at most4/3,
attained at p=1/2. For fixed B-prefix preparation and two marginal-preserved
continuations, the expected extra proposal cost is(B-2) times the mean
prefix cost, in addition to two full native costs and coordination/replay.
Thus even an ideal coupling cannot improve successful pairs per unit
expected cost if that ratio exceeds4/3. The observed native costs here are
diagnostics, NOT a universal expected-cost theorem or statistical estimate.

The evidence defeats the proposed cheap ROOT boundary on these cases; it
does not refute V6/V10's written coupling proofs, every other prefix feature,
or all dLLM tasks. Preserve it, do not replace these cases with favourable
examples or count cancelled forward work as free. No large root-proxy
campaign follows. The scientific publication/usefulness goal stays OPEN.
