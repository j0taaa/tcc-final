# Classical reduction and all-bank decision

The registered law oracle checks ALL40 integer partitions of pools2/4/6/8.
Random-pair class-first pivotal allocation has the same marginals and atom
diagonals. It differs from minimum-repeat matching in six profiles, with
only one difference at B=6: (1,1,2,2). For its two singleton classes,
matching joint probability is1/30 and pivotal is1/27. Cross big/small values
are1/15 and7/108 respectively. These exact distinctions do not establish a
useful speed/variance advantage. No positive-covariance obstruction was
found in these finite cases; this does not prove pivotal globally safe.

ALL six OLD banks are retained, with their32-forward fitting costs. The new
matching6 bias-variance reductions relative to iid2 are2.18--5.50%, on known
64-observation banks only. Pivotal6 is nearly identical and has slightly
lower bias variance in five of six; the largest absolute difference in
relative reduction is0.00002454. Retained RSS still has lower scalar-score
variance in all six. Same-proxy analytical CV wins on five scores and loses
on one, as before. None of these calculations is an original-population
result, an independent benchmark, total timing, or full-parameter training.

**Decision:** do not promote matching6 as a practically better decoder or
start a large neural campaign from this proxy. The possible contribution is
the exact safety/query theorem, whose originality and CFG-specific useful
application still need to survive review. Mechanistic novelty is weak: the
law is very close to a competent classical pivotal design. Preserve both.
