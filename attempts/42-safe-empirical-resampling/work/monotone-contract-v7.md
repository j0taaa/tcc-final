# V7: a universal covariance refinement of the classical four-pool law

2026-10-11. Written BEFORE a new numerical probe or sampler. V6's pure proof
stands, but its balanced frontier is NOT universal domination of classical
C4. No new neural campaign or favourable benchmark is authorized by this
contract. Earlier practical losses remain preserved.

1. **Use.** Two expensive scores/gradients or two full continuations of
   native dLLM/CFG transitions prepared from one common frozen head. Six
   cheap original proposals, two selected continuations; literally retain
   first. Classifier, native control and parameters fixed before current
   draws. GLC validation/fallback/copies/setup remain part of each proposal.
2. **Difference.** Investigate a covariance refinement which dominates the
   actual classical C4 law for EVERY class probability and EVERY target
   function, rather than only balanced worst directions or iid2. Conditional
   PSD refinement and pivotal/matching principles are known. Exact sharp
   source-query/covariance tradeoff is the candidate, not those principles.
3. **Why choose.** Never worsen C4's terminal pass@2 or any projected score
   variance; strict if conditional class means differ. Six-source overhead
   still needs a meaningful cheap-proposal/expensive-continuation use. Pivotal,
   retained RSS, root stratification, mass-aware and larger-batch controls
   remain competent; do not claim superiority over all of them or pass@1.
4. **Without code.** Compare the conditional class law of matching6 with
   C4 on a random four-subpool retaining first. At profile(3,3) the difference
   is a negative class-contrast rank-one form. At every other profile keep
   C4. Average multinomial counts for an explicit negative weighted graph.
   Investigate whether mixing this safe law with matching6 at fixed1/L
   dominates C4 for ALL probabilities with at most L classes. This last
   statement is a CONJECTURE until proved/refuted, not a theorem.
5. **Validation.** Exact independent class-law and raw-tape small oracles;
   spectral search is a counterexample aid, never proof or model evidence.
   Publish the exact adverse C6-versus-C4 example. Freeze any producer before
   registered rerun. No changes to independent corpus/protocol or metrics.
6. **Cost.** Six original draws/classifications, fifteen tiny matchings or
   a uniform three-of-five subpool and three tiny matchings, exact integer
   selection, two full continuations, setup/output/cache costs. No credit
   for avoided forwards before actual native source boundaries are audited.
7. **Objection.** Conditional all-function refinement may have tiny additional
   benefit; the fixed blend may fail. Classical pivotal or rank designs may
   realize the same law/frontier. Preserve counterexamples, not just improved
   atoms. A cheap verifier may prefer evaluating all six proposals. Expensive
   full-trajectory proposal generation would invalidate the cheap-source use.
8. **Publication.** Need exact distinctive guarantee/priority, complete proof,
   meaningful source-versus-continuation cost and independent application.
   A reference/oracle is insufficient. This is an isolated mathematical
   refinement; main article and maintained APIs are unchanged.
