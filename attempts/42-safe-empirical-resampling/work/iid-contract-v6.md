# V6: arbitrary IID source, pure analytic proof and directional frontier

Written before a new sampler/oracle. This refines42, preserving v1--v5 and
their negative strong controls; it is not a fresh favourable model campaign.

1. **Use.** A pair of original noising-score/stochastic-gradient evaluations
   for a fixed CFG-valid JSON/DSL candidate and fixed dLLM parameters. Generate
   six cheap original corruptions IID, with replacement, classify by a finite
   fixed cheap proxy, retain the literally first corruption, and evaluate only
   it and its minimum-repeat matching partner. Unlike v4, repeated ORIGINAL
   states are permitted. Count-NELBO/native continuous-time sources are known
   antecedents; the target expectation is unchanged.
2. **Difference.** Investigate an exact unconditional all-vector covariance
   proof for this IID-pool operation with NO finite population certificates,
   monotonicity assumption on model gradients, or known class probabilities.
   Matching, pivotal, RSS, and basic variance identities are classical. The
   candidate contribution is a safety/query tradeoff theorem, not claiming
   their principles or a unique mechanism. V5's near-classical law stands.
3. **Why choose.** Candidate: positive protection on EVERY nonconstant class
   mean direction, plus sharp worst-direction efficiency at balanced class
   masses under a universal opaque-IID-proposal budget. Classical paired RSS
   has rank-one covariance reduction; it may still win for scalar scores.
   Pivotal6 can share the balanced frontier. Mass-aware, ordered, analytical
   and mask/count-specific controls remain allowed in applications.
4. **Without code.** Derive the IID joint polynomial as the multinomial
   limit of the written v3 counting formula; take the ANALYTIC B0>=0 argument,
   not v4's computer-assisted boundary, to a sum-of-squares inequality. Prove
   raw conditional-class residuals have zero cross covariance. Derive the
   balanced minimax directional bound from Bernstein completeness and trace.
   Write hypotheses/cost/CFG noising corollary before a new implementation.
5. **Validation.** Exact symbolic/rational small oracles only. No new model
   benchmark or favourable instance selection. All earlier banks/controls
   remain negative for practical promotion. Avoid claiming the directional
   frontier bounds algorithms supplied known class masses or non-IID queries.
6. **Costs.** Six native noising states and classifications, fifteen tiny
   matching candidates, one bounded integer selection, two expensive scores
   or gradients, output/storage and any proxy fit/update. Native count-mask
   generation does NOT require the LCM ticket lifting used in v4. If callers
   use original tickets, preserve them; do not deduplicate their aliases.
   Classifier fitted only from past remains independent of current draws.
7. **Objection.** The theorem may be known under two-phase/pivotal/RSS theory.
   Balanced masses are an analysis case, not prior information given to the
   universal algorithm. Between-class variance may be negligible in neural
   gradients; a correct guarantee then has little practical benefit. Output
   bias has cheap target-histogram analytical controls; do not omit them in
   any future full-cost claim. No full-gradient learning has been validated.
8. **Publication gap.** Need the actual distinctive theorem/priority audit,
   a meaningful CFG-specific application and honest complete-cost benefit.
   A pure proof is better evidence than a finite numerical probe, but does
   not itself meet the user's whole goal. Main article/API remain unchanged.
