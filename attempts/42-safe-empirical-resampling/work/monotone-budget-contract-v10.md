# V10: a monotone, all-budget refinement of the classical four-proposal law

2026-10-11. Written before this refinement's oracle or measurements. V9's
endpoint sufficiency remains OPEN. An exploratory all-even matching screen
has no obstruction; it is not a theorem and is not used below. V10 changes
the construction rather than assuming that conjecture.

1. **Use:** two expensive loss/gradient evaluations of corruptions of a
   CFG-valid JSON/DSL target, or two continuations of the same native decoder
   transition. Extra original proposals must actually be cheap. Retain the
   first literally; keep the classifier/parameters/control fixed beforehand.
   A gain in a pair statistic is not a trained model or latency victory.
2. **Difference:** investigate an explicit universal covariance refinement
   of the competent C4 coupling at EVERY proposal budget B>=4, monotonically
   improving as B grows. Matching6 does not dominate C4 universally. Recycling
   diagonal mass, Dirichlet forms, U-statistics and conditional Jensen are
   classic; the particular bounded-source family/quantified frontier is the
   candidate result, not those principles. It may still be too incremental.
3. **Choice:** preserve original marginals and never worsen ANY projected
   score variance or terminal pass@2 against C4, despite a bad fixed proxy.
   Strictness and extra-proposal costs need explicit conditions. Pivotal,
   retained ranked-set sampling, mass-aware couplings, CV and extra independent
   full evaluations remain strong competitors, not forbidden adversaries.
4. **Code-independent:** start with C4's conditional class joint law on a
   random four-subpool. Let d_i be its conditional diagonal and subtract the
   graph Laplacian with weights min(d_i,d_j)/(L-1), with fixed declared L.
   Prove marginal/nonnegativity, unconditional covariance domination, and
   budget monotonicity by U-statistic conditional expectations and concavity
   of min. At balanced masses derive its limit and compare the all-coupling
   trace bound. This is not an all-budget proof of minimum-repeat matching.
5. **Validation:** exact independent four-position matching enumeration,
   rational complete raw-tape small cases, and exact balanced count sums.
   These are correctness/proof aids, never favourable model benchmarks.
   Preserve every mismatch and earlier failures. No neural protocol here.
6. **Complete cost:** B original draws/classifications, O(B+L) counts and
   first-row integer weights, integer categorical draw, two expensive original
   evaluations, classifier setup and output. Charge additional proposals.
   Known class masses/inverses can be cheaper; no opacity claim then.
7. **Objection:** the refinement may be a direct known survey-sampling or
   Markov-kernel construction; the balanced convergence may be much slower
   than atom-optimal methods. A finite worst-direction bound does not make
   it optimal for a particular neural score. More proposals can lose on cost.
8. **Publication:** need primary-source differentiation AND a meaningful
   CFG/dLLM complete-cost application before promotion. The written theorem
   or passing oracle alone does not satisfy the user's scientific goal.

Scope: IID original sources WITH replacement, finitely many fixed labels
0,...,L-1, B>=4, square-integrable target; ignore current target values in
selection. Independent positions retain aliases/repeated raw states. No
model calls or maintained API/article change is authorized by this note.
