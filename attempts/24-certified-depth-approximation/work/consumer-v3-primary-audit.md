# Primary audit and direction selection — 2026-10-16

No new model forwards in this written stage. Goal remains active.

Mosaic's official artifact is now available and pinned at
`36945e4b3c64913e23962e0924a8b40a84c58807`:
https://github.com/MhDang/mosaic . The README, matcher, JSON builder,
tokenizer conversion, and sequential/parallel samplers were read locally.
Its existing engine-facing consumer asks for the full joint sample and the
sampled-token marginals. Its arbitrary-JSON sub-schema uses a fixed depth;
typed finite schemas bound themselves. This locates a possible actual role
for A24 and supplies a substantially stronger operational baseline than the
CPU-only V2 comparisons. No V2 claim is upgraded to a native GPU victory.

Other directions considered before new implementation:

- Templates/independent string gaps: [Template Infilling](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.284/)
  and [DIA](https://github.com/Westlake-AGI-Lab/DIA) already exploit anchors.
  Their public descriptions/README were read. A lexical-only control may
  solve that operation without recursive grammar inference. No novelty is
  claimed for templates or for manufacturing a JSON-only need around them.
- Returning an expected numeric confidence rather than a sampled number is
  not a new principle. [SECL §3.2, Eq2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09624v1)
  already uses the expected digit bin; [Merdjanovska et al. §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.04899v1)
  already sums digit probabilities and explicitly discusses the AR
  conditional-digit cost and its approximation. Those sections were read.
  A global probability-sum constraint may require further inference, but
  knapsack/expectation-semiring inference is classical. No new large readout
  implementation or neural campaign is justified solely by Jensen's inequality.
- [Future Validity §4–5](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07698v1) provides approximate
  future-validity error accounting for an AR/speculative target. Its setup,
  stated bounds/estimator hierarchy and finite-language scope were read;
  this is not a claimed verification of every appendix proof. It reinforces
  that conditioning/future-mass error bounds are antecedents. AR native
  trajectory conditioning must not be conflated with A24's frozen-product law.

Selected next step: a SMALL correct bridge to Mosaic's unchanged samplers,
followed by a separately frozen real-consumer/resource protocol. The candidate
scientific content remains A24's specific closing certificate and its actual
operational consequence. Sufficiency of that distinction is still a real
obligation, not established by finding a repository or running an adapter.
