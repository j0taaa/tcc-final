# A24 consumer V3 — existing serving consumer and mandatory GPU controls

2026-10-16. Written before the new adapter, new neural heads or new timings.
The user has explicitly requested continuous work until the positive scientific
goal is met. V2 is complete and remains a limited pilot; it is not relabeled.

1. **Real consumer.** Mosaic's published `ConstraintMatcher.propose_x0` is
   already consumed by diffusion decoding loops and serving integrations.
   Its `JSONBuilder.any_json` bounds an empty sub-schema by `max_depth=3`.
   Investigate an A24 certificate for that finite-depth approximation to the
   complete original-token JSON posterior. This is a concrete consumer of
   joint samples and confidence, rather than a new task requiring that API.
   A raw fixed-canvas sampler is only the first correctness preflight; an
   eventual live claim must cover the actual model/termination policy.
2. **Scientific difference.** Weighted abstraction, depth bounds, tensorized
   automata, sparse queries and compilation reuse are established. The
   candidate is the specific token/lexer closing certificate already written
   in A24, now tested as an operational error contract for an existing engine.
   No new theory of parsing, automata or Rao–Blackwellization is claimed.
   The novelty/significance objection remains open and must not be hidden
   by implementing an adapter. A substantive real-use benefit is necessary.
3. **Why choose it.** A fixed depth alone supplies no error bound against
   unbounded JSON. A24 supplies U/(L+U), and can select a smaller sufficient
   depth when closure mass is small. Compare against BOTH published Mosaic
   samplers, native fp32 and its fp64-product configuration, matched compact
   CFG/stack/counter controls, and equal compilation/cache opportunities.
   Never discard Mosaic as an opponent just because it uses floating point.
4. **Written guarantee.** The existing conditioning/coupling proof and the
   certificate-separation family remain the theory. The graph adapter proof
   in `consumer-v3-adapter-proof.md` must precede its code. It concerns the
   language and real-arithmetic posterior, not IEEE sampling exactness.
   Token bytes/aliases, internal depth peaks, fixed IDs and final-number
   flushes must agree. Native Mosaic tokenizer/EOS contracts are audited
   separately; do not silently replace joint byte decoding by per-token text.
5. **Honest stages.** First establish small original-word and marginal
   correctness with no real forward or speed claim. Then freeze a resource
   and consumer protocol on independently selected external inputs, before
   new model evaluation or golds. V2's old heads may diagnose compatibility,
   not independently confirm useful latency. Do not enlarge a failed pilot
   or change its threshold after inspecting results.
6. **Complete cost.** Count tokenization, byte table, automaton construction,
   minimization, dense/sparse buffers, host/device copies, all forwards,
   certificates, sampling, confidence, restarts and output. Model/dtype/
   runtime/cache/precision contracts must be recorded. A24 and controls may
   all compile once and reweight compatible canvases. Dense GPU storage,
   fp64 capability and finite precision can determine usefulness.
7. **Strong objections.** A classical compact CFG or the native GPU sampler
   may already be faster. A typed schema or a lexical-only infiller may
   avoid recursion entirely and is an obligatory task comparator when
   applicable. A generic JSON template must not be chosen just to exclude
   such a control. Learned correctness/calibration is not implied by syntax
   or by TV to another decoder. Finite-time managed outputs need their own
   FAIL contract. Existing negative results remain valid in their scopes.
8. **Article gate.** Need an identifiable original construction/application,
   full written proof/cost, meaningful independent operational benefit,
   competent native controls and a software consumer that needs the result.
   An adapter, native replay, test suite or capability refusal alone does not
   meet this gate. Editorial acceptance and mestrado admission are external.
