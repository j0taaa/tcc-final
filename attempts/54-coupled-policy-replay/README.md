# A54 — Coupled comparison/replay of grammatical reveal policies

2026-10-16. WRITTEN before prototype, new score statistics, heads or timings.
A52's reward-gradient application and A53's simple terminal screen remain
negative/closed. This is a DIFFERENT operation: evaluation of two predeclared
policies on the same frozen neural backbone, sharing identical model inputs.

1. **Real use.** A researcher compares two reveal-policy configurations on
   structured dLLM generation. Each must keep its declared individual rollout
   law; the evaluations may be correlated. Identical canvases/model inputs
   need only one frozen-backbone forward. A planned comparison or an unseen
   independent validation-replay set is the consumer. No improvement of
   semantic answer quality/pass@1 or automatic variance reduction is claimed.
2. **Difference.** Candidate enabling detail: A52's tractable OBSERVED-action
   mass permits maximal coupling after hidden proposal/anchor integration.
   Coupling itself, maximal agreement, speculative replay and memoization are
   CLASSICAL. Coupled LLM evaluation is published at AISTATS2026; DARE already
   uses tokenwise maximal coupling in its error analysis. Own sufficient
   significance/priority of this structured-action application remain OPEN.
3. **Why choose.** Full observed-action coupling has pointwise maximal
   diagonal mass. It can retain shared inference prefixes more often than
   couplings required to share a complete latent proposal or lexical class
   path. That is an information-contraction comparison, not a new coupling
   principle. Must beat optimized common random numbers, word-conditioned
   maximal MASK coupling, class-conditioned maximal ACTION coupling, ordinary
   exact compiled inference and identical-input memoization on all controls.
4. **Proof first.** work/theory-v1.md gives marginal correctness, prefix-only
   shared-work optimality within the explicit faithful/co-adapted contract,
   contraction and an infinitesimal L1 opportunity identity. Global MEXIT,
   reunion caching, semantic-score variance and total latency lie outside that
   theorem. MEXIT's nonfaithful look-ahead constructions are stronger controls
   when their future-kernel information/cost is available, not impossible.
5. **First validation.** ONLY small rational joint-table/marginal/contraction/
   cache oracles and a counterexample to unrestricted optimality. No new model,
   policy update, auxiliary or real-score calculation at this stage. Any later
   OLD-data diagnostic/full-cost campaign needs its own written protocol and
   exact source/input freeze. No native finite-RNG equality inherited from A52.
6. **Complete costs.** All head/grammar/normalizer construction, class sums,
   action queries, exact integer bit work, residual draws, replay storage,
   copying and synchronization, initial/reference and new evaluation costs.
   Maximal residual rejection has heavy tails even with bounded expected draw
   count. Finite caps produce FAIL, not an unbiased arbitrary fallback. A
   zero-extra-cost local advantage is not a full-cost win. Equal-input caching
   is mandatory for EVERY comparator, including after reunions when valid.
7. **Objections.** Class-conditioned coupling may already recover almost every
   useful match. Added partitions may cost more than the saved forwards.
   Changed backbone weights cannot share a forward. Raw-token finite-machine
   law must be explicitly defined/verified: FP mass formulas cannot simply be
   inserted into an exact accept/reject claim. A policy adaptively chosen from
   the SAME exposed draft invalidates its assumed proposal distribution.
   Require policies fixed independently of replayed drafts, or actual valid
   conditional laws; no silent on-policy/training-reuse claim.
8. **Article gap.** Sufficient own distinction from prior coupling/evaluation,
   real marginal-law boundary, finite complete-cost benefit versus ALL strong
   controls, external inputs and independent confirmation. No native decoder
   speed/quality, human review or publication/admission claim. First only a
   small mathematical oracle; do not build a broad replay system from algebra.
