# A63 — Optimal stopped work for an unknown-law three-class cap-six coupling

2026-10-17. Eight criteria BEFORE prototype, LP solve or model work. A38/A42
motivate the question, but their native pilots are not evidence for A63.

1. **Use.** An iid black-box oracle returns complete original payloads with a
   known three-valued observable (including failure when applicable). Return
   an antithetic pair retaining the literal first and both full marginals.
   Such an operator can wrap an original structured decoder or a simulator;
   the class probabilities need not be known even when individual logits are.
   A concrete new consumer/full-cost benefit has NOT been validated. Do not
   impose an artificial two-output restriction on ordinary best-answer search.
2. **Specific candidate.** Determine the least expected source calls at the
   uniform three-representative law among ALL universal cap-six algorithms
   attaining every atom's known Bernstein-optimal collision reduction. This
   is not invention of empirical antithetics, polynomial factories, LP duality
   or the cap-six diagonal. The possible new result is a sharp stopped-work
   constant and an explicit attaining rule. Priority/significance are OPEN.
3. **Why choose it.** A full six-draw empirical matching is a competent existing
   achiever. A rule using strictly fewer than six calls with the same atom
   optima and same common-score covariance could reduce real oracle work.
   No saving is presumed. Strong binary factories when only two labels are
   ALLOWED, probability-aware transport, larger pools, iid budgets and
   score-aware designs remain practical controls outside this exact contract.
4. **Proof first.** work/contract-and-lp-v1.md defines the operator, classical
   degree-six Bernstein constraints, finite stopping-flow LP and symmetrized
   fair-law lower-bound reduction BEFORE computing. A rational primal AND
   dual certificate are required before claiming an optimum. No pointwise
   optimality for every P or finite-RNG/neural theorem is inferred.
5. **First validation.** Only a minimal independent finite rational LP audit.
   Freeze source before solving; preserve primal/dual/status and any failed
   reconstruction. Exact constraints must pass, with zero duality gap. Then
   check the stopping rule against independent full-tape enumeration and
   direct six-pool laws on small rational distributions. Constructed laws are
   proof/correctness objects, not neural workloads or favorable benchmarks.
6. **Complete costs.** Count full original calls, failed returns, key work,
   coordinator work, rational arithmetic, memory and pair consumption. Cost
   optimized first is source-query count, NOT wall/CPU/evaluation efficiency.
   Returning a nonfirst original index gives the independent within-class
   payload lift. No hidden extra forward or replay credit. A later real
   application requires a separately frozen complete-cost protocol.
7. **Objections.** Universal output law alone may already have a known optimal
   factory. Label-specific algorithms must not be excluded from the lower
   bound; symmetrization is valid only for the uniform comparison point.
   Fewer source calls need not beat iid or more-pool variance/work. Three
   ALLOWED classes is different from a larger alphabet with three observed
   classes. Full raw-payload optimality beyond the representative-law lower
   bound must not be assumed. The LP may find no saving or fail certification.
8. **Article obligations.** Need a surviving sharp result, nearest-source
   priority review, independent proof audit and useful real operation. No
   neuron/head/weight/update/label/timing or publication claim. Do not promote
   a solver result, a small sampler or a resource screen as goal completion.
   If the exact LP reveals no own benefit, preserve it and change direction.
