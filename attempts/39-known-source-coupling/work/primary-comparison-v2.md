# A39 primary-source objection: finite-source no-free-lunch

2026-10-11. Actual reading scope, not a claim of exhaustive priority.

Ren, Zhao and Ermon, *Adaptive Antithetic Sampling for Variance Reduction*,
ICML2019/PMLR97:5420--5428. Official
[paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/ren19b/ren19b.pdf) and
[supplement](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/ren19b/ren19b-supp.pdf).
Read main introduction, Sections2--3,5 and conclusion; supplement Section8.1
(proof of Theorem1). Main PDF SHA256
`680e97e3e59331608e18f445914aad51d33f342eb3d01b0b0fbb7df0d798c17c`;
supplement `30efe9eb8ea1bf01241fbf7993f3b5c24985996c8f91f7554ddb0975da045deb`.

Theorem1 ALREADY proves the uniform finite-source worst-function variance
ratio lower bound `1-1/(M-1)`, attained by uniform sampling without replacement.
Its spectral/trace proof permits arbitrary marginal-preserving couplings.
Therefore A39's universal no-worse-than-iid covariance and its source
derangement correction cannot be presented as a new general antithetic
principle, or a uniformly substantial improvement for all final verifiers.
The proof is consistent with A39's preserved `(2,1,1)` counterexample.
The main general construction learns a Gaussian source correlation adapted
to a specific integrand; its training and function information differ from
an equality-query-only universal class-collision operation.

The specific candidate difference is an opaque-label QUERY problem with
known addresses: sharp cap3/cap4 robust per-atom attainable risk and sharp
worst-placement expected query cost among simultaneous robust maximizers,
plus sound-coarsening actual-query omission and a native zero-helper-forward
boundary. The inspected theorem does NOT state these query-cost results.
This is a scoped difference, not proof of novelty by absence. Rank pools,
auxiliary omission, finite-population corrections and coefficient clipping
remain established antecedents (the original scopes and hashes preserved
independently in A38 v8 can be consulted; no mutable code is imported).

The meaningful native claim must involve a real final consumer affected
by the cheap decision partition, with complete extra sampling/parser/copy
costs measured. Claiming only tiny `1/(2^32-1)` seed duplicate avoidance
would not meet the user's scientific/usefulness requirement. Cheap
derangement, seed-half, more iid paths and biased novelty remain controls.
No learned antithetic baseline may be declared defeated without running
an aligned operation and charging its preparation fairly. Priority,
competitive application and publication sufficiency remain OPEN.
