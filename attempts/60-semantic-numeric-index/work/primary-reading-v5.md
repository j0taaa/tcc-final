# V5 primary reading and scope

2026-10-17. The numerical construction/lower bound was writtenf4c373d before
prototype321582c. These are read scopes, not an exhaustive priority search.

* [Jiraskova/Okhotin2008, State complexity of cyclic shift](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1051/ita:2007038.pdf),
  Section2 including Lemma1 and Theorem1/proof were read directly. It attributes
  the middle-state union of suffix/prefix concatenations to Maslov1970 and
  gives the quadratic nondeterministic construction. This ALREADY supplies the
  unweighted source-cut idea; marking a unique e/E cut and commuting scalar
  path weights is a specialization, not a new closure principle. Its later
  lower-bound sections were not read here; do not conflate them with V5's
  written one-way INPUT/OUTPUT numerical-normalization lower bound.
* [Gorman/Sproat2016, Minimally Supervised Number Normalization](https://aclanthology.org/Q16-1036.pdf),
  Sections4.1/4.1.1--4.1.5 were read directly. It already composes numerical
  factorization, numeral maps, verbalization grammars and lexical transducers;
  discusses ambiguity and dynamic programming. Its described target is number
  names, not the semantic quantile operator. That scope observation does not
  establish novelty of V5 or invent finite-state numerical normalization.
* [Machida/Shibakov, Stack and register complexity of radix conversions](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.08878),
  Introduction and Clinger-problem Definition2/Corollary1 were read directly.
  Its central obstruction is approximation between incommensurable radices.
  V5 keeps exact base-ten scientific semantics without floating-point rounding;
  no radix-conversion result is being overturned or re-proved as a new theorem.
  The remaining30-page proofs have not been read here.
* [From Hadamard expressions to weighted rotating automata and back](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2018.09.017)
  was located via its publisher abstract/introduction only. Full method/proofs
  remain an antecedent to inspect; do not claim a missing weighted-rotation
  feature from the abstract or count that paper as fully audited.
