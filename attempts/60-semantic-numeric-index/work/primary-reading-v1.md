# Initial primary scope and mandatory comparisons

2026-10-17, before implementation. No exhaustive priority claim.

* [Piskorz/Kobalczyk/van der Schaar2603.02913v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.02913v1),
  Introduction, Section2.1 and AppendixB.3.4 were read directly. It estimates
  numerical statistics from100 autoregressive samples and trains regression
  probes on hidden representations. These are established numerical-statistics
  consumers. They do NOT have A60's frozen mean-field target law. No equality
  with its final autoregressive distribution or empirical advantage is assumed.
* [Mohri2009, Weighted Automata Algorithms](https://www.cs.nyu.edu/~mohri/pub/hwa.pdf),
  Section5.1/Equation23 and the epsilon-filter discussion, PDF pages13--17,
  were read directly. Generic weighted composition, lazy construction and
  constant-state epsilon filtering already provide the projection machinery.
  Naively interleaving epsilons can multiply probabilities. A60 cannot claim
  this algebra, laziness or epsilon correctness principle as new.
* [Doleschal et al., The Complexity of Aggregates over Extractions by Regular
  Expressions](https://lmcs.episciences.org/11712/pdf) was located/opened,
  but its quantile theorems/proofs have NOT yet been read. It is an outstanding
  substantive antecedent, not excluded by its abstract or a search snippet.
* [Regression Language Models for Code](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/akhauri26b.html)
  abstract/metadata only were inspected. The direct official PDF fetch through
  the web tool returned Internal Error. No full-paper claim or missing-feature
  assertion is based on that retrieval failure.

The A59 corpus remains a conformance FIT audit, not a newly confirmed numerical
prediction workload. Its code is not imported as mutable machinery here.
Before a resource/model campaign, inspect complete cached register quantiles,
generic/lazy WFST semantic projection and the outstanding quantile literature.
Matching a classical compact construction would defeat a generic new-method
claim even if a small prototype is correct. No campaign is triggered here.
