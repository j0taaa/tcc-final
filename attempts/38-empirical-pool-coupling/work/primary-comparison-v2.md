## Exact reduction, not merely resemblance

The separate test oracle randomly permutes the observed class blocks in a
four-draw empirical sample, uniformly chooses the first draw's rank within
its block, and rotates the rank by two. Its canonical joint law equals A38's
full law EXACTLY on the independent rational alias/failure case. Thus the
basic law IS grouped empirical rank coupling, not a new sampling principle.
Neal1995's original reflection and the general exchangeability framework
remain direct antecedents. The putative difference is the SHARP adaptive
expected-cost optimum4-2c+s3 under universal categorical cap4, not the law's
existence. Do not equate that distinction with priority confirmation.

## Probability-aware controls can be stronger on ALL scores

The ordinary half-circle rotation is a maximum-diversity control even if
it has positive covariance for some nonmonotone predicates. That weakness
does NOT exhaust informed methods. For uniform P on m categories, drawing
the second uniformly among the m-1 different categories preserves P and has
Cov(f(X),f(Y))=-Var_P(f)/(m-1) for EVERY score. It has zero canonical
collisions, better than A38, and needs no auxiliary iid samples if P is
known uniform. Keep this control; do not claim that informed schemes must
trade away the universal covariance guarantee.

More generally, a valid informed comparison can be constructed by choosing
zero-sum vectors v_i. If all p_i<=1/2, a closed polygon with edge lengths p_i
gives ||v_i||=p_i and sum v_i=0. Then

    J_ij=p_i*p_j-<v_i,v_j>

is nonnegative by Cauchy--Schwarz, has row/column sums p_i, zero diagonal,
and covariance -(||sum_i f_i*v_i||²)<=0 for every score. If m=max p_i>1/2,
use collinear vectors with heavy length1-m and each other length p_i,
oppositely oriented; the heavy diagonal becomes2m-1, all others0. This
achieves the full minimum collision AND universal nonpositive covariance.
These are geometric/transport principles, not a claimed new A38 invention.
Constructing/sampling the informed law and computing its exact probabilities
cost; that cost must be included, not presumed infinite or zero.
