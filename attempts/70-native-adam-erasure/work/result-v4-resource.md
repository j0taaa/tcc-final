## Eight-criterion decision after measurement

1. Reproducible lossless probabilities are a real use, but this particular
   CPU/FP32 component has insufficient optimistic full-cycle savings.
2. Specific optimizer-erasure priority/significance remain unconfirmed;
   classical beta1=0 skipping, interval erasure and finite-state jumps stand.
3. The required20% resource advantage fails before adding any A70 overhead.
4. V1's declared-recurrence written proof remains, without transfer to an
   unimplemented PyTorch/Apex/LibNC adapter.
5. All predeclared records/control modes/frames are retained; no selection,
   extra workload or independent positive branch is triggered.
6. Full component cost was measured, with preparation/validation separated;
   complete file compression and actual inactive-gap demand were not measured.
7. Small optimizer share is fatal to THIS screened application; do not infer
   that large embeddings elsewhere automatically satisfy gaps/native identity.
8. No article promotion. A DIFFERENT actual consumer, actual native/read
   compatibility, useful erasure frequency, strong full-cost benefit and
   sufficient own priority would still be required for substantive expansion.
