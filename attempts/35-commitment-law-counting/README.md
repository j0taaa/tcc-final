# A35 — Probability of a commitment is not a CFG posterior

2026-10-11. WRITTEN companion investigation, before implementation. The goal
is to justify when exact probability-free coordination of native dLLM commits
has a real computational role. This is not a neural benchmark or a claimed
new theory of model counting. A34 frontier remains a separate frozen attempt.

## Eight questions before code

1. **Use.** Batch two original-law structured dLLM continuations from a frozen
   head and grammar. Native compatibility decisions can couple slots even when
   their proposals were independent. A binary class is determined by an actual
   accepted dispatch tag, rather than a token the parser may reject. Software
   consumes complete JSON/DSL variants and arguments. The mathematical family
   uses checked versus unrestricted dispatch with a witness tag; it is a proof
   object, not a dataset built for favorable speed or quality measurements.
2. **Difference.** #DNF, probabilistic-inference hardness, unambiguous CFG
   parsing, and proposal/conditioning differences are classic. Candidate bridge:
   exact probability of an ACCEPTED tag of a polynomial greedy/MWPC commitment
   can be #P-hard even for a compact LL(1) regular grammar whose frozen-product
   posterior is easy. This is a narrower observation than generic hardness,
   and may be a direct corollary rather than publishable alone. Its purpose
   is to substantiate A34's unknown-probability operation; the combined sharp
   discrete-output frontier still needs specific-priority/significance review.
3. **Choose.** A probability-free pair can give certified original marginals
   and nonnegative coverage gain without computing this class mass. The A34
   theorem is optimal ONLY for regular sample-only access, not every algorithm
   reading logits/RNG/grammar. Compare native iid2/iid3/iid4, raw-randomness
   stratification, exact or approximate density-aware methods, and CFG posterior
   decoders as different-law practical controls. Hardness does not prove physical
   speed, output quality, or necessity of calculating p for every coupling.
4. **Mathematics.** Specify an ideal categorical-ranking/frozen-head kernel,
   confidence order, LL(1) grammar, original aliases and actual accepted tag.
   Reduce #DNF to its exact probability, by an explicit rational identity.
   Prove polynomial feasibility/commitment and contrasting posterior formula.
   Explain why original greedy and primary-objective MWPC give the SAME tag
   event in this family. It is not yet an exact finite-CUDA EPIC theorem.
5. **Experiments.** Only tiny independent grammar/policy enumeration, once
   the proof is attacked. No synthetic timing campaign or favorable semantic
   benchmark is authorized by this proof object. A real application would need
   input-only public selection and frozen complete-cost controls BEFORE heads.
   The negative A32 evidence is preserved, never reused as an A35 success.
6. **Costs.** Account for grammar size, witness alphabet encoding/tokenization,
   slot domains, aliases, ranking generation, all feasibility checks and bit
   precision. #P applies to exact mass evaluation as a FUNCTION of arbitrary
   grammar inputs, not a fixed tiny deployment or approximation. A34 consumes
   extra transition draws but only two expensive continuations; more-output
   iid may win. No additional forward is assumed free until a faithful native
   transition wrapper establishes that boundary.
7. **Objections.** Tagged sum-type constraints can conceal a trivial witness
   enumeration; posterior sums witnesses easily, greedy counts their UNION.
   Do not conflate them or deliberately use ambiguous syntax. Full random tapes
   and approximate masses can outperform the sample-only contract; no lower
   bound is claimed for those. A35 may just instantiate classic random-output
   counting and may not increase publication impact. No semantically useful
   accepted partition has yet been demonstrated on external model data.
8. **Article.** Need primary perturb-and-MAP/probabilistic-program/#DNF priors,
   independent mathematical audit, a faithful native adapter, full-cost utility
   and a coherent main statement. No reviewer/novelty/acceptance is invented.
   Keep this small as a companion; reject if it merely hides a worse comparator.

Contract: one ideal frozen head, original finite token domains (zero probability
outside declared IDs, not silently pruned), literal feasibility/MWPC objectives,
no EOS or full-trajectory advantage unless separately derived. The native
upstream currently uses unbounded text gaps in check_valid; the proof isolates
fully fixed assignment suffixes to avoid using an unwarranted finite-gap claim.
