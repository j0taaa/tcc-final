[Snijders1984](https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/1005009268/Statistica_Neerlandica_-_June_1984_-_Snijders_-_ANTITHETIC_VARIATES_FOR_MONTE_CARLO_ESTIMATION_OF_PROBABILITIES.pdf),
Sections2--4 and AppendixA, credits classical variance reduction. Section3's
lemma minimizes convex moments of an integer variable by concentrating on
adjacent integers; its theorem gives the minimum variance of a fixed-size
Bernoulli output sum for given marginal probability. AppendixA characterizes
optimal indicator tuples through stratification/CDF transforms. Thus A34's
integer convex-envelope ingredient and Fréchet objective are NOT inventions.
The written candidate distinction is the joint OUTPUT-pair minimax frontier
with unknown p, sequential sample-only input, and worst EXPECTED draw budget,
including attainability. That specific query was not the theorem in the
sections inspected; this does not prove publication priority. Web retrieval
reads the original PDF; local HTTP403 and screenshot error are not silently
reported as successful local/visual inspection.

[Craiu--Meng](https://utstat.toronto.edu/craiu/Papers/backforw.pdf),
Sections1--3.3, directly states costs can offset variance gain. Theorem1/2
propagate negative association through monotone update maps and monotone
estimands, with additional limiting conditions for backward coupling. Those
are close precedents for trajectory claims. A34's binary class conditional
lift is a different sufficient condition: its covariance is minus a square
of conditional means for ANY single common real-valued score, without a
monotone raw-noise/decoder map. This elementary conditional-covariance lift
is NOT alone claimed new. No guarantee for different unrelated scores,
arbitrary adaptive local energy improvements or universal physical latency.

These sources strengthen the precedents rather than establishing novelty.
