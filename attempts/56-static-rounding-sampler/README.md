3. **Why choose it.** Potentially fewer arithmetic operations/transfers than

   dual-directed kernels, exact rational and other applicable exact controls;
   approximate Mosaic/sparse GPU remains a legitimate cost/accuracy alternative.
   Do not compare only with the rejected ticket/scalar algorithm or slow CPU.
4. **Without code.** First prove a guarded positive-circuit enclosure theorem,
   quotient/scale semantics, zero propagation and retained-prefix sampling.

   every numerical guard, GPU synchronization, constants/enclosures, random
   bits, sampling, aliases, CPU fallback and output. A precompiled exponent is
   not free cold preparation. If intervals are materialized, do NOT claim half

7. **Objection.** This may be ordinary forward-error analysis implemented on
   GPU with no significant own advance. Guards/coarse bounds/fallbacks may cost
   more than two directed values. Equal layout optimization may remove a win.
   Most applications may prefer ordinary approximate arithmetic. If no useful
   own distinction survives, stop before a broad neural campaign.
8. **Article still missing.** A primary-source distinction, substantial measured
   benefit over strong same-operation controls, a real complete consumer and
   independent evidence. No new sampling/CFG theory, priority, human review,
   native EPIC superiority or achieved user goal is claimed. Passing a tiny
