# Original sources and limits before code

2026-10-17. Research reading, not a comprehensive priority review.

- Original Nacu/Peres2005, *Fast simulation of new coins from old*,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0309222 : Table1, all Proposition22/23 proofs
  and Section8 Questions1--3 read. They relate stopping moments/uniform tails
  to smoothness. No claim that a2005 question remains unsolved today or that
  generic regularity analysis is new. Our proposed distinction fixes the
  preselected-batch interface; it is not a resolution of their whole Question3.
- Original Koskela/Karvonen/Latuszynski/Spano2510.01941v2,
  https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01941v2 : introduction including the explicit
  nonadaptive interface, Theorem1/Remark1 and the already-read implementation/
  open-problem scope. They credit Keane--O'Brien and Mendo for nonadaptive
  predecessors and distinguish adaptive factories. No new claim to that
  distinction, their genealogy application or existence of nonadaptive rules.
- The original2024 genealogy consumer and Morina2021/Lee etal importance
  framework were read in A68. This note does not import mutable A68 code or
  turn its failed numerical gate into a positive application.

Local A34's written uniform-curvature argument was read. The new proof is
self-contained; the use of stopped scores/Wald is classical. Searches for
nonadaptive/adaptive moment and regularity separations did not identify the
specific result, which DOES NOT confirm priority. A current full literature
audit and a natural useful consumer are still required before promotion.
