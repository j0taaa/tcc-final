# Additional nearest-proof audit during the frozen V25 experiment

The measured program/protocol is unchanged. This note records primary-proof
reading, not independent human review, an exhaustive priority certificate or
an acceptance prediction. The V24 negative result remains decisive against
the earlier inexpensive-control comparison.

## Fu: the actual compression proof, including its normalization appendix

Primary: <https://arxiv.org/html/2609.24235v1>, Section3.2's entire Lemma3.5
proof and AppendixA's entire LemmasA.2/3.4 argument, in addition to the main
admissible-gate statements already recorded. AppendixA uses approximate l1
Lewis weights, repeated square roots and a matrix inverse square root in the
declared real-arithmetic model. It supplies bounded-aspect-ratio normalization,
not a hidden sharper planar endpoint construction. Lemma3.5 explicitly uses
a lattice direction net of size O(eta^-K), brute-force nearest-net assignment
and linear angular-loss accounting. Its K=2 instantiation therefore does not
state the endpoint O(eta^-1/2) core or the ordered product bound developed here.

This verifies the particular published upper-bound comparison; it is not a
lower bound on Fu's approach or a claim that every possible specialization of
it must incur that cost. Dudley's planar quadratic rate and the general
composition principle remain classical. A49's candidate contribution is the
explicit integer construction and resulting quantitative algorithm, not any
one of those ingredients or the first polynomial-bit TV algorithm.

## Domain reduction: all fixed-tree WTA proof passages

Primary: <https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18707v2>, Sections3.1/3.2,4.1--4.3 and
5, including Section5's fixed-tree weighted-tree-automaton reduction and its
proof. The randomized compression theorem uses dimension-dependent Lewis
sampling, preserves bilinear products and linear sums, and normalizes by
exact bottom-up partition functions. Determinism of the probabilistic circuit
is NOT necessary. Weighted latent alternatives are not a reason to dismiss
this competitor.

The fixed common tree is its stated structural hypothesis. Different parse
splits of a general word chart need not have a common v-tree; that observation
does not show an impossibility of a better encoding. Moreover, A49's ACTUAL
explicit-stack prototype is a fixed-slot chain with a compatible common tree.
Thus the implemented examples cannot claim a capability unavailable to that
paper. Only the deterministic quantitative guarantee distinguishes the
operation in this comparison. No randomized runtime comparison has been run.

## Consequences for the manuscript

The theoretical product bound can be assessed without choosing a favorable
model example. It improves the stated deterministic upper bounds that were
read, but novelty sufficiency still needs a careful complete claim and review.
For grammar conditioning, source admission must be paid separately: the
written chart construction is polynomial; the measured explicit-stack bridge
is not an implementation of that construction and can refuse larger inputs.
Neither a positive precision gate nor geometric oracle success closes that
implementation gap or establishes superiority of an entire dLLM decoder.

The current new corpus gate must finish unchanged, including ALL refusals and
losses. Any new optimization is development, with equally improved controls;
it cannot inherit independent-confirmation status from V25.
