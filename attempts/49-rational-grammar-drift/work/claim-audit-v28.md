# Consolidated claim and proof obligations

This is an internal adversarial audit of the V27 draft, not peer review or a
mechanized proof. The external feedback is useful advice, not a certificate
that a human expert checked all lemmas. No new publication/EPIC claim.

## One protagonist

The candidate result is an explicit deterministic rational TV algorithm with
an improved precision-cost upper bound. For ordinary products the claimed
core arithmetic cost is O(nq sqrt(n/epsilon) log(q+2)); for full-state Markov
paths it is O(nq^2 sqrt(n/epsilon) log(q+2)). Both include the separately
specified input/scaling/normalizer/output operations and polynomial bit cost.
For an admitted general event forest the bound is different; for source
LL(1) input its chart construction and grammar/byte parameters must be paid.

Do not defend a new geometry rate, first rational/bit-polynomial TV algorithm,
first admissible-gate propagation, first grammar conditioning, first coupling
budget, or a new decoding policy. A24's applied query advantage is a separate
result and must not be added to A49's guarantees or attributed to this core.

## Exact comparison with primary results

Sources checked again on 2026-10-12:

- [Feng, Liu and Liu](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.14696v1), Theorems1/2,
  product sparsification proof and the Markov recurrence: published arithmetic
  upper bounds are O(q n^2 epsilon^-1 log(q) log(n/(epsilon TV))) and the
  q^2 Markov variant. Their q-way heap is an antecedent. The epsilon
  convention must be converted consistently. Their polynomial bit capability
  is already credited in the draft; it is not a distinction of this work.
- [Fu](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.24235v1), Lemma3.5, Theorem3.8,
  Corollaries1.4/1.5 and AppendixA. Latest abs listing still has v1. The
  explicit direction net contains O(eta^-K) generators and brute-force
  assignment costs O_K(m eta^-K); K=2 gives the stated epsilon^-4 product
  corollary. Our endpoint construction yields O(eta^-1/2) generators and
  linear ordered processing. Fu already supplies TV support realization,
  signed admissible-gate composition and removal of a positive-TV floor.
- Dudley and Matousek1996 provide the classical planar quadratic geometry
  and constructive bit-model antecedents, as recorded in V27. Replacing Fu's
  compressor by suitable classical geometry may recover the improvement;
  our candidate contribution is the fully charged sharper resulting algorithm,
  not a claim that the replacement principle is unprecedented.
- [Statistical similarity](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.10527v3), current
  version v3 (v2 is withdrawn), abstract, Sections2--5 statements and
  proof framework: it approximates S=sum min(P,Q)=1-TV relatively. The current
  v3 uses likelihood-ratio sparsification (Section5/Algorithm1); the earlier
  v1/anonymous search hit instead uses masked knapsack/branching programs.
  Do not attribute that old construction to the latest version.
  Relative accuracy for S does not automatically
  imply relative accuracy for TV near zero. An additive error at most eta
  in S gives only additive eta in TV; to resolve a budget tau with margin
  gamma*tau it requires eta of that order. This is a relevant alternative,
  not a newly discovered hardness result or an excluded competitor. The v3
  statements, Algorithm1, running-time calculation and main Lemmas9/10 proof
  passages were read; its AppendixC has not been fully reconstructed. This
  note does not independently certify every estimate in its proof.
- Domain reduction and inference-based randomized relative approximation
  remain competent alternatives as recorded in V25/V27. A deterministic
  contract is a distinction of guarantees, not proof that randomization
  is useless or that these algorithms are slower on the captured heads.

This establishes a specific comparison with published expressions examined,
not exhaustive novelty or a lower bound on the approaches above. A targeted
search with no matching sharper statement cannot certify priority. The core
quantitative claim is substantial enough to review, but publication
significance must not be replaced by test counts or the number of attempts.

## Practical consequence and stop conditions

The certificate bounds every event probability of the compared valid-word
laws, hence can audit a supplied fidelity budget. It does not validate a
quantized network, eliminate the forward needed to obtain the two heads,
or make neural generation faster automatically. The numerical application
rounds probability tables; the original full-V masks and tokens are retained.

V28 examines every budget interval and all45 old pairs. Improvements in
those intervals are conditional opportunities, not proof that software needs
one of the observed budgets. A deployment risk policy must be supplied
independently, and saving must exceed all certification costs. The illustrative
decimal grid is not a customer requirement or a replacement for V25's failed
gate. Do not initiate another independent neural campaign merely because
an endpoint region exists. Consolidate the theorem and decision evidence;
keep the actual use and novelty gaps explicit.
