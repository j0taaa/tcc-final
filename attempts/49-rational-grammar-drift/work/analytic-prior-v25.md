# Additional primary elimination check — Analytic product-TV proxy

Checked2026-10-12 during the frozen independent audit, with no method/protocol
change. Primary: Avital, Kontorovich, Vershynin and Zou,
[arXiv2609.21049v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21049v1),17 September2026.
Read Sections1/2 (both statements and proofs) and3.2's tensorization corollary;
not a complete bibliography audit or exhaustive priority certificate.

Theorems1.1/1.2 give a dimension-free constant-factor characterization of
product TV, reduced to one scalar equation by Latała's theorem. This is a
competent alternative if constant accuracy suffices. It does not state a
deterministic near-relative1+epsilon algorithm or a grammar-conditioned law.
After grammar conditioning the token coordinates need not be independent;
the independent midpoint construction cannot silently be applied to marginals.
No exclusive product-TV approximability or first scalar proxy is claimed here.

This does not establish A49 novelty. It records another closely relevant
antecedent, and prevents describing near-relative computation as the only
way to estimate a useful drift. Human judgement/acceptance remain absent.
