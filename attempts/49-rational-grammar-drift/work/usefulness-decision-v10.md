# Decision: consolidate the quantitative mathematical candidate

2026-10-12. This is a research judgement with stated evidence, NOT independent
human review, acceptance prediction or absolute literature-priority proof.

## What someone can use, and why choose it

Operation: compare two frozen product heads conditioned on the same recursive
grammar, retaining original token identities. Return deterministic rational
lower/upper bounds on TV with arbitrary requested relative precision. A
software consumer can audit probability-table storage/rounding against a
bounded-verifier drift budget. The upper applies to every verifier in[0,1],
not just favorable labels, chosen JSON documents or an observed completion.
Classical conditioning can magnify tiny table perturbations, so independent
token-error measures alone do not answer this operation.

Choose the method when inexpensive Hellinger/range upper bounds cannot
certify the budget and a deterministic relative certificate is required.
The six-case real full-head audit illustrates this: FP16 epsilon0.1 returns
U=4.095231914338327e-5 versus Hellinger0.0027838314673018887. These data were
not used to choose cases, but this one packet is not an independent competitive
corpus or general empirical superiority. Every format/tolerance remains in
the generated report. The cheap controls are faster and should be tried first.

## What changed mathematically

Classical geometry/inference are the ingredients. The explicit integer
endpoint construction and logarithmic exact queries give O(sqrt(d/epsilon))
stored rows, O(H(d/epsilon) log(d/epsilon+2)) bounded-leaf forest core work,
and O(nq sqrt(n/epsilon) log(qn/epsilon+2)) ordinary-product arithmetic work.
Every integer remains polynomial-bit; no ideal real normalization primitive
or conditioning-mass/positive-TV floor is assumed. A trimmed LL(1) chart
construction charges full grammar/source preparation. It is not the current
software normalizer or an invented parsing result.

This improves the deterministic PRECISION-COST UPPER BOUNDS of the inspected
SODA2024/Fu2026 results; it is more than an empirical constant speed-up on
selected states. It does not imply an algorithmic lower bound, exclusivity,
optimal TV computation or speed dominance over a strengthened planar method.
Exact small enumeration and randomized approximations remain competent.

The direct code and small independent checks support correspondence to the
mathematical recurrence; the proof establishes the asymptotic claim. It is
not necessary to obtain a favorable decoder benchmark for that claim to hold.

## Scientific sufficiency and remaining risks

There is now a concrete quantitative result worth developing as an algorithm
paper/TCC candidate, rather than a negative demonstration or a generic parser
application. Specific closest-prior comparisons, complete proof and full
input-bit boundary are in the seven-page mathematical draft. The draft is
separate from the stable A24 main article until the remaining adversarial
checks settle it. Do not restart unrelated samplers merely to seek a win.

An expert may regard the planar specialization of Fu/Dudley as too direct
for a particular venue, or locate a prior sharper TV bound. Current searches
do not disprove those possibilities. Independent proof reading and academic
judgement have not occurred. Those facts limit any claim of publication
certainty; they do not erase the explicit mathematical improvement relative
to the primary upper bounds actually examined.

No EPIC generation advantage, trained/quantized neural model, improvement in
semantic accuracy or universal physical latency has been demonstrated. A
common continuation policy and uniform reachable-state bounds are required
for trajectory TV; confidence-threshold changes are not automatically covered.
Probability-table nominal storage is not a measured whole-model memory saving.

Continue with adversarial proof/nearest-prior checking, remove avoidable
algorithmic costs on small exact inputs, preserve every refinement and freeze
the reproducible draft. Do not relax claims or build a favorable benchmark.
