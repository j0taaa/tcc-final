# Before-code primary reading and limits

2026-10-17. Not a systematic novelty review; no contact or human review.

- Nacu--Peres2005 ORIGINAL arXiv math/0309222v5: read Proposition10 and its
  proof, Proposition22, Table1 and Questions1--3 in Section8. Bernstein
  envelopes/elevation and smoothness questions are known. Proposition10's
  C2 construction only has a1/n tail bound; the text itself distinguishes
  that from finite expectation. Do not claim generic factory existence or
  the positive-mixture principle as inventions.
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0309222
- Holtz--Nazarov--Peres2011 ORIGINAL arXiv0808.1936v3: read Lorentz recurrence
  equations27--29 and polynomial reproduction, Lemma22 and the displayed
  consistency construction's use of unspecified constants. Their general
  smoothness/factory theorem already provides polynomial tails, including
  exponents giving finite mean. This is NOT an unavailable capability. Full
  theorem/proof reading recorded earlier in A34 is separate historical scope;
  no exclusion of an implicit quantitative specialization in the rest of the
  paper is claimed here. https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.1936
- Peter Occil AUTHOR'S CURRENT problem note (dated2026-10-17): read main
  question, efficiency, conjecture/update and strategies. Explicit Bernstein
  coefficients, computable error constants and coefficient consistency are
  actual requested constructions; a function-error bound alone does not
  establish consistency. The page explicitly reports a counterexample to a
  broad implication, so do not rely on that implication.
  https://peteroupc.github.io/bernreq.html
- Same author's original derivations/table: read order-two Boolean sum,
  Lorentz coefficients and PropositionB10C/proof. The Boolean-sum error
  constant is already explicit for Hölder f''; its operator evaluates f on
  the full n+1-point grid. This is a strong control, not a claim that all
  earlier coefficients lack effective bounds.
  https://peteroupc.github.io/bernapprox.html
- BernoulliFactory.jl AUTHOR repository documentation was found; indexed
  function list is a lead to a real library interface, not a claim that its
  complete current implementation was audited or lacks a particular feature.
  https://github.com/awllee/BernoulliFactory.jl

Sevy1991/Draganov and other quasi-interpolation sources were only search or
reference leads in this round, NOT original full-text reads. Their existence
prevents a broad “first derivative-free correction” claim. No priority follows
from missing a keyword match. A substantial implicit-reduction comparison
remains required before a large implementation or article promotion.
