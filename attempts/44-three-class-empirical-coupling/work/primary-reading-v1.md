## Originals actually read in this audit

Jafari Jozani/Perron2011 *Antithetic Sampling*, DOI10.1007/s13171-011-0004-2,
author report <https://www.usherbrooke.ca/mathematiques/fileadmin/sites/mathematiques/documents/Recherche/Publications/Rapports_de_recherche/rr64.pdf>:
introduction, Section2 construction and Theorem2.1/proof, Section3 inclusion
representation and Theorem3.1/Corollary3.2/proofs. The record in A34/A41 also
preserves earlier readings. This paper already separates cheap ranking from
expensive measurement, uses a shared iid pool, and gives variance bounds.
The n=2 antithetic construction chooses sorted ranks(1,4) or(2,3) from4
queries. It is not generally the same as cyclic half-shift on THREE groups:
on sorted labels(a,b,b,c), that reflection has a b,b diagonal, whereas the
minimum-atom half-shift pairs a,b and b,c. Their Theorem2.1 proof uses order
of the measured scalar; imperfect proxy rankings and arbitrary gradients
require an explicit lift rather than silently declaring G ordered.
Their Section3 has broader inclusion/spacing comparisons. A44's root argument
is not a claim to invent unbiased subsampling, rank antithetics or those bounds.
